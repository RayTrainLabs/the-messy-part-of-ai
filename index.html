What moves it

Where this breaks

Three things I'd get wrong if I oversold this

01
Google says it is still SEO Google's own guide, May 2026: no special markup and no llms.txt needed for its AI features. The fundamentals you already pay for still count. Don't buy a second programme to replace them.
02
AI visitors don't automatically convert better The largest study so far, 973 shops in Marketing Science 2026: Google organic converted about 13% better than ChatGPT referrals. The value is the shortlist, not the visit.
03
The numbers move, and most of them are sold to you Answers change from run to run. Most AI visibility data comes from companies who sell tracking, and that includes us. Measure your own: the same questions, five runs, every month.
