# The no code prompt pack

From the Raygency webinar *The messy part of AI: Production, ROI, and Your Visibility*, 6 November 2026.

Everything here runs in a chat window. No code, no account beyond a free assistant.

## The one rule

**Test like a buyer, not like the brand owner.** Logged out or in a private window. A fresh chat for every question. Never type your own brand name. Run each question five times, because the answer changes from run to run.

## Step 1. Write your twenty questions (10 minutes)

Paste this into any assistant. Fill in the brackets.

```
I sell [what you sell] to [who buys it] in [country or region].
Write 20 questions a real buyer would type into an AI assistant
before they have a brand in mind. Mix them:
5 about a problem they have, 5 comparing options,
5 about how to choose, 5 about price or value.
Do not name any brand in the questions. Write them the way a
person types, not the way a marketer writes.
```

Keep the list. You will use the same twenty every month, or the trend means nothing.

## Step 2. The shortlist test (the one from the webinar)

```
What are the five best [your category] brands for [your kind of customer]?
Give one reason for each.
```

Record: were you named, in which position, and with what reason.

## Step 3. Run your twenty, and keep score

For each question, in a fresh chat, record in a sheet:

| Question | Run | Engine | Named? (y/n) | Position | Who else was named | Sources it cited |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Your **presence score** is the share of answers that name you. Five runs of twenty questions is one hundred answers per engine, so the score reads straight as a percentage.

## Step 4. Find out why a rival wins

When a rival is named and you are not:

```
You recommended [rival] for [the question]. What specifically about
[rival] made you include them? List the sources or kinds of sources
that support that, as specifically as you can.
```

What comes back is usually a review site, a comparison article, a Reddit thread or a specific statistic on the rival's own page. That is your to do list.

## Step 5. Check what it says about you

```
What do you know about [your brand]? What is it best known for,
who is it for, and what are its weaknesses? Cite sources.
```

Look for anything wrong or out of date. Wrong facts in the answer usually trace to a wrong fact on a page somewhere. Fix the page.

## Step 6. Write the sentence you want quoted

Pick the five pages buyers already land on. For each, paste the opening paragraph and ask:

```
Rewrite this opening so an AI assistant could quote it as a fact.
Keep it under 50 words. Replace every vague claim with a specific,
checkable number or a named source. Do not invent numbers: where
I need to supply one, write [NUMBER NEEDED] and tell me what it is.
```

The GEO study (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024) measured plus 41% visibility from adding quotations and plus 31% from adding statistics. Keyword stuffing showed no lift.

## Step 7. Repeat monthly

Same twenty questions, same engines, five runs, same week each month. Change one thing at a time between runs so you know what moved the number.
