# Questions for your team

From the Raygency webinar, 6 November 2026. Take these into your next marketing, SEO or leadership meeting. Each one has what a good answer sounds like, so you can tell a real answer from a confident one.

## Where we stand

**1. When a buyer asks an AI assistant about our category without naming us, how often are we in the answer?**
Good answer: a percentage, from a fixed set of questions, run several times, with a date. Weak answer: "we rank well on Google, so we should be fine."

**2. Which assistants did you check, and were you logged out?**
Good answer: at least ChatGPT, Google's AI features and one more, in a private window. Weak answer: one run, in a logged in account.

**3. Which rival is named most often when we are not, and why?**
Good answer: a name, and the kind of source behind it (a review site, a comparison article, a forum). Weak answer: "they spend more on ads."

**4. Is anything the assistants say about us wrong?**
Good answer: a list, with the page it probably comes from. Weak answer: "we haven't looked."

## What we are doing about it

**5. Which five pages do buyers land on first, and does each one open with a sentence an assistant could quote as a fact?**
Good answer: the five URLs and a date to rewrite them.

**6. Where are we mentioned on sites we don't own?**
Good answer: review platforms, press, comparison articles and communities, and a plan to earn more. Mentions correlated more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks in Ahrefs' 75,000 brand study (0.664 against 0.218).

**7. Are we paying anyone for "AI optimisation" that Google says is unnecessary?**
Google's own guide, May 2026: optimising for its AI features is still SEO, and special files or markup are not needed for Google. Ask what the spend is for.

## How we will measure it

**8. Do we have a GA4 channel for AI assistants, and is it above Referral in the channel order?**
Good answer: yes, with the date it was set up.

**9. Are we using Search Console's generative AI report?**
Good answer: yes, and we know it shows impressions only, with no clicks or queries.

**10. Does our form or checkout ask how people heard about us, with AI assistants as an option?**
Good answer: yes. Assistants often strip the referrer, so analytics undercounts them.

**11. What is a shortlist appearance worth to us, in money?**
Good answer: a model on our own numbers, labelled as a model. Use the ROI worksheet in this kit.

**12. What would change our mind?**
Good answer: a named number and a date. "If presence has not moved in three monthly runs after the page rewrites, we stop and look again."
